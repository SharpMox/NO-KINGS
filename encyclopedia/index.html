Fairy Chess Pieces

A visual reference for the movement of fairy chess pieces — non-standard pieces used in chess variants and chess-problem composition. Pieces are grouped by how they move: pick a tab to explore each family. Sourced from Wikipedia's List of fairy chess pieces.
