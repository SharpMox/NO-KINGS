Inversions

A reference of the 14 inversion pairs in NO-KINGS.

These pieces look the same after a 45° ortho ↔ diagonal swap (or are otherwise self-inverse), so they have no distinct inverted form. They're listed here for completeness.

14 Pairs of pieces that invert to one another — a 45° ortho ↔ diagonal swap of every atomic component.
