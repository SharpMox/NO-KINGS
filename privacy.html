Privacy

What leaves your device, what does not, and how to remove it.

The short version. NO KINGS has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking. Everything the game stores stays on your device unless you choose to sign in to Google Play Games — and even then, your saves go to your own Google account storage, not to us. We never receive them.

What the game stores on your device

The game keeps its state in your device's private application storage. Nothing here is transmitted anywhere, and it is removed when you uninstall the game.

  • The current run — board state, score, wave, and what you are carrying.
  • A local high-score table and a short history of recent runs.
  • Your settings, such as sound and animation preferences.

What happens if you sign in to Google Play Games

Signing in is optional. The game is fully playable without it, and declining costs you nothing but cloud backup and leaderboards. If you do sign in:

DataWhere it goesWhy
Your Google Play Games player ID Stays with Google. The game reads it to know which account a save belongs to. So a save restored on a new device is yours and not someone else's.
Your save data (a few kilobytes) Your own Google Drive application-data folder, via Play Games Saved Games. So progress survives a reinstall or a new phone.
Your score and Play Games display name Google's leaderboard for this game. Only if you post a score to the leaderboard.

In every case Google is the party actually holding the data, under Google's own privacy policy. Sharpunk operates no server for this game and holds no copy of your saves, your account identifier or your scores.

Data leaving the EU

Google processes this data on infrastructure that may sit outside the European Union. That transfer is governed by Google's terms and safeguards, not by ours, because the data never passes through our hands.

What the game does not do

  • No analytics or telemetry SDK of any kind.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifier is read.
  • No crash-reporting service.
  • No social, attribution or marketing SDKs.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data — there is none to sell.

How long anything is kept

Local data lasts until you delete it or uninstall the game. Cloud saves and leaderboard entries live in your Google account for as long as you keep them.

Removing your data

  • Local data: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in your device settings.
  • Cloud saves: delete them from the Play Games app, under your profile's saved-games list for NO KINGS.
  • Leaderboard entries and your Play Games profile: managed in your Google account, since they belong to Google's service rather than to us.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, erase and port your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because Sharpunk holds none of your data for this game, requests about saves, scores or your player profile are answered by Google, and the routes above are the fastest way to act on them.
